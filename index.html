Built for one more try

Pure arcade. No distractions.

Just the essentials that make an arcade shooter hard to put down. No loading between rounds, no accounts, no unlocks—just you, your ship, and a whole lot of rocks.

Rotate, thrust, drift, fire and wrap around the playfield. Fly smart or get caught in the debris.

Waves keep coming

One large asteroid on wave 1, up to ten starting on wave 10. Difficulty stops scaling there, but the fight keeps going.

Original arcade audio

How the game plays

Three lives. Destroy asteroids to earn points, and pick up an extra life every 10,000 points.

Speed matters. Your ship carries momentum, and the screen wraps at the edges. Smaller asteroid pieces move faster.

There's no end to the waves. Starting with wave 11, each new wave gives you exactly four seconds of flashing collision protection.

Your next round is waiting
